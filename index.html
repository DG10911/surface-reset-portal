CARS24 Automated Machine Stations

In real-world Cars24 Mega Refurbishment Labs (MRLs), vehicles move along a continuous automated line powered by six distinct specialized machines. Click Solo demo on any machine to inspect it in 3D, or run the full line in Sim.

01
Station 01 · 3D LiDAR Profiler t = 0 — 1.9 s · Machine 1
Technology Solid-state LiDAR + structured-light cameras
Output 3D envelope map, contour calibration
Duration 0 – 1.9 s
Action on heavy soil Routes to 60 s pre-cycle → 22.6 s standard
Capex share ₹2.2 L (vision + edge compute)

The scan data is the first and only measurement Cars24 gets on the vehicle. Every downstream module is parameterised from it. Heavy soil is triaged before it enters the cycle — protecting the 22.6-second promise.

02
Station 02 · 25kV Ion De-Static Chamber t = 1.9 — 4.7 s · Machine 2
Technology 25kV high-voltage pulse ionizing bar array
Output Electrostatic charge neutralized, particulate unbonded
Duration 1.9 – 4.7 s (overlaps mist at 3.8 s)
Air velocity 18 ± 2 m/s laminar flow
Capex share ₹0.9 L (ionizer bars + transformer)

Dust bonded by static charge is the hardest contamination to remove without water. Ionized air breaks the electrostatic bond before any contact is made — so the microfiber array lifts clean instead of smearing.

03
Station 03 · Polymer Encapsulator t = 3.8 — 7.5 s · Machine 3
Chemistry Rinseless nano-polymer encapsulant
Volume / car < 2.0 L total liquid, 0 L rinse water
Duration 3.8 – 7.5 s (overlaps ion at 3.8 s, textile at 5.6 s)
Function Polymer encapsulates soil particle → safe to wipe
Capex share ₹0.9 L (mist manifold + dosing pump)

Zero rinse water is the single biggest scale unlock. Every Cars24 hub already has a lane — but none have an effluent pit for a tunnel wash. Polymer mist replaces the water flood with chemistry. No drain retrofit required. Footnote: 0 L rinse at the portal; textile laundering + mist make-up water are accounted in opex.

04
Station 04 · Robotic Textile Machine t = 5.6 — 15.1 s · Machine 4 (FLAGSHIP)
Technology Segmented conformal microfiber array
Coverage Roof + both sides simultaneously
Duration 5.6 – 15.1 s · longest, highest coverage
Stroke 1.80 m compliance stroke per segment
Capex share ₹4.5 L · largest single BOM item

This is the innovation. Sequential detailing does roof, then door, then bonnet. The conformal array does all of them at once. Simultaneous microfiber contact vs 4 minutes of sequential hand work. The other 5 modules exist to make this one work at 22.6 seconds.

05
Station 05 · Air-Knife Drying Gantry t = 11.3 — 18.8 s · Machine 5
Technology High-velocity centrifugal air-knife blowers
Function Strip residual moisture, no drying tunnel
Duration 11.3 – 18.8 s (overlaps textile from 11.3 s)
Air gap 120 mm knife gap
Capex share ₹2.4 L (blowers)

A tunnel dryer adds 60–90 seconds and requires a permanent structure. Air-knife sheets running alongside the textile array mean the car exits dry without any additional drying stage. The 90-second door-to-door time depends on this.

06
Station 06 · SiO₂ Nano-Coating Finisher t = 19.5 — 22.6 s · Machine 6
Chemistry SiO₂-based spray sealant, air-knife leveled; full cure off-line
Output Gloss, surface uniformity, hydrophobicity
Duration 19.5 – 22.6 s · final 3.1 seconds
Gloss estimate 58 GU → 88–94 GU (simulation)
Capex share ₹0.6 L (gloss applicator)

The outbound car looks better than it did when it arrived. SiO₂ is not just aesthetics — it is the product quality proof. A glossmeter at QC gives the hub GM a number to show customers. This is what converts reconditioning from a cost centre to a revenue line.
